      ⚠ One missable trophy — read this first

      Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable trophy in the game. Lucia invites you to meditate with her on a balcony in Chapter 1, and that invitation is gone once Chapter 1 ends — no mission replay, no New Game Plus. Do it the moment it is offered, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave). Jump to its guide ↓

      Roadmap to the platinum

      One playthrough is enough — no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, and no online trophies. GTABase estimates the platinum at around 200 hours; Push Square puts it at 300-plus. Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session, so keep them off your trophy save. Follow the five stages in order.

        After the credits, the 100% Completion Checklist unlocks and becomes your clean-up list. Everything outside the story stays available after the story ends.
